Privacy Policy

Last updated: 22 November 2026

OzyKlips is a personal tool used by a single creator to upload their own videos to their own TikTok account.

What data we use

  • TikTok account data: when the account owner authorizes OzyKlips, TikTok provides an access token, a refresh token, the account's open ID and basic profile information (display name). These are used only to upload the owner's videos and to show which account is connected.
  • Videos: the video files the owner chooses to upload.

How it is stored

Tokens are stored only on the owner's own private server and are never sold, shared or sent anywhere except to TikTok's official API. Video files are deleted from the server after upload or within 3 days.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect data about other TikTok users or viewers.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising or tracking.
  • We do not share any data with third parties.

Deleting your data

Revoke OzyKlips' access in TikTok (Settings and privacy, Security, Manage app permissions). Stored tokens then stop working and are deleted. You can also ask for deletion through the contact link below.
