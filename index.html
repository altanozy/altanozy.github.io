OzyKlips

A personal clip publishing tool

OzyKlips is a small, private desktop tool used by a single creator to publish their own edited, vertical gameplay highlight clips.

How it works

  1. The creator edits a short vertical clip on their own computer.
  2. The creator reviews and approves the clip manually.
  3. OzyKlips uploads the approved video file to the creator's own TikTok account using the official TikTok Content Posting API. The video arrives in the creator's TikTok inbox as a draft, and the creator finishes and publishes it inside the TikTok app.

OzyKlips is not offered to the public, has no other users, does not post to anyone else's account and does not collect data from TikTok users.
